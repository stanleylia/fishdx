Result artefacts
----------------
The files in results/*.json are archival artefacts. They do not each embed a
per-run git_commit field. Each headline artefact is regenerable by the script named
in REPRODUCIBILITY_MAP.md sections 3 and 9, operating on the cached embeddings
in results/_cache_*.npz.

Evaluation of record vs intermediate artefacts: see results/README.md.

Implementation and producing code
---------------------------------
The paper's headline numbers are produced by the named analysis scripts in
scripts/experiments/ over the cached Florence-2/CLIP embeddings. The src/fishdx
package provides the aligned end-to-end implementation; see
REPRODUCIBILITY_MAP.md section 1 for the equation-to-code mapping.
