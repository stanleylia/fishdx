## Clinical presentation

A healthy fish under aquaculture observation presents uniform pigmentation with no haemorrhagic or ulcerative lesions, intact scales without loss or ruffling, clear eyes without exophthalmia or opacity, fully extended and undamaged fins without fraying or erosion, regular schooling or typical species-specific behaviour, steady opercular rhythm without gasping or flared opercula, no cotton-like or fluffy growths, no discrete white spots or red patches, no abdominal distension, and a responsive feeding reflex. In practice the healthy state is defined negatively (absence of disease markers) combined with positively observable normal behaviour.

## Lay-visible appearance

A healthy fish has uniform body colour with no red marks, no white patches, no fuzz, no swelling, and no visible parasites. The scales are intact, the eyes are clear, the fins are fully extended without damage, and the fish swims normally and feeds actively.
